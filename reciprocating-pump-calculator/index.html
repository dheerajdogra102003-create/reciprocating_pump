Calculator Capabilities & Analysis Features

Core computational engine and automated data reduction workflow.

◆ Predefined Apparatus Constants

Calibrated cylinder bore (55 mm), stroke length (40 mm), collecting tank area (0.1 m²), and energy meter specifications (3200 pulses/kWh) are built directly into the reduction pipeline.

◆ Three-Run Multi-Head Workflow

Process three experimental test runs simultaneously across Low, Medium, and High operating head regimes with instant side-by-side comparative diagnostics.

◆ Automatic Hydro-Mechanical Reduction

Evaluates electrical input power (Ei), motor-to-pump shaft brake power (Es), actual discharge (Qact), theoretical swept volume (Qtheo), total head (H), and hydraulic power (Eo).

◆ Comprehensive Efficiency Evaluation

Accurately computes Overall Efficiency (ηoverall), Volumetric Efficiency (ηvol), and Pump Hydraulic Efficiency (ηpump) with live numerical indicators.

◆ Interactive Performance Characteristic Curves

Dynamic HTML5 canvas charting engine plotting Head vs. Efficiencies, Head vs. Discharge, and Head vs. Power curves with responsive coordinate scaling and legends.

◆ Formal Reports & CSV Data Export

Download comprehensive text lab reports, generate Excel-compatible CSV files, or print pre-formatted laboratory evaluation sheets with student verification fields.
